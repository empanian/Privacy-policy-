Overview

The short version

Bottom lineWe collect only what is needed to run your account and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

OmniChat is a Chrome browser extension that lets you broadcast one prompt to multiple AI services simultaneously. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the rights you have over it.

01

What we collect

Email addressTo create and identify your account. Used for login and password reset emails.
PasswordStored as a secure bcrypt hash — never in plain text, never readable by us.
Daily usage countNumber of broadcasts per day, stored server-side to enforce free-tier limits.
Brain memoryText you manually type into the Brain tab, synced to your account across devices.
ProjectsProject names and descriptions you create, synced to your account.
Conversation historyPrompts and AI responses from your broadcasts, stored locally and optionally synced.
IP addressLogged temporarily at registration to limit abuse. Not stored long-term.
License keyYour Whop subscription key, used to verify Pro access each session.
02

What we never collect

  • AI conversation content
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising cookies
  • Payment card details
  • AI account credentials
  • Device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Data for ads
API KeysAny API keys you enter are stored only in Chrome's local storage on your device. Never transmitted to our servers.
03

How we use your data

AuthenticationYour email and password let you log in securely across devices.
Cross-device syncBrain facts, projects, and conversation history sync via Firebase so your data is available anywhere.
Usage enforcementDaily broadcast count enforces the free tier of 5 broadcasts per day.
License verificationYour key is checked against Whop each session to confirm your Pro subscription.
Abuse preventionIP rate limiting prevents mass account creation to abuse the free tier.
04

Third-party services

We use these services to operate OmniChat. Each handles data under their own privacy policy:

🔥 Firebase (Google)
Authentication and Firestore database. Your account and synced data lives here.
firebase.google.com/support/privacy
💳 Whop
Payment processing and license management. We never see your card details.
whop.com/privacy
☁️ Render
Backend server hosting. May log request metadata (IP, timestamps).
render.com/privacy
🔒 No AI services
We share nothing with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or others. Their own policies govern your use of them.
05

Security

Password hashingbcrypt with salt rounds — we cannot read your password.
TransportAll communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
Token expiryAuth tokens expire after 30 days and require re-login to refresh.
Local dataAPI keys and conversation cache stay in Chrome's local storage, never sent to us.
Database rulesFirebase security rules ensure each user can only access their own data.

07

Children's privacy

OmniChat is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

08

We may update this policy as OmniChat evolves. When we do, we update the effective date above. Continued use of OmniChat after changes means you accept the updated policy.
